Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for an internship? +
You can apply by selecting your preferred domain and completing the interest form provided on our website.
What details are required in the interest form? +
Basic personal details, educational background, and your area of interest are required to submit the form.
I have submitted everything. What happens next? +
After verification, shortlisted candidates will receive further instructions via email.
What is the duration of the internship? +
Internship duration usually ranges from 4 to 8 weeks depending on the selected domain.
When will I receive my internship certificate? +
If tasks are submitted before the deadline, certificates are issued during the final week of the following month.
